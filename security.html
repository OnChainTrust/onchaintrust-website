Security Features

Comprehensive protection for every layer of your on-chain interactions.

Kill Switch

In reaction to incident reports, our Kill Switch feature allows DApp owners to discourage users from interacting with their website during active security incidents or hacks. Instant response to protect your users.

Phishing Attack Prevention

Customer education is important, but it doesn't protect against frontend and DNS hacks. OnChainTrust provides automated protection at the wallet level.

Front-End Attack Prevention

We automatically protect against interactions with malicious contracts on verified domains. Even if a DApp's frontend is compromised, your transactions stay safe.

Clipboard Hijacking Prevention

We protect against attacks that track and change your local clipboard for blockchain addresses. Never accidentally send funds to the wrong address.

How DApp Security Works

A simple three-step process that keeps your users safe from the moment they connect.

1

Domain Verification

DApp owners register and verify their domain and associated smart contract addresses with OnChainTrust.

2

Real-Time Monitoring

Our system continuously monitors for discrepancies between verified contracts and what's presented to users.

3

Instant Protection

When a threat is detected, users are warned before signing any malicious transaction. Kill Switch can be activated instantly.

Auto-Detect Known Scams

OnChainTrust operates one of the largest public databases of labeled smart contract addresses. We help you as a second line of defense in detecting potentially unsafe addresses.

  • Community-reported scam addresses
  • Real-time blacklist updates
  • Cross-chain address verification
  • Integration with major threat databases
